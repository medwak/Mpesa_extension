Upload your M-Pesa statement

Upload the PDF statement Safaricom emails you directly, or a CSV with the statement columns (Receipt No., Completion Time, Details, Paid In, Withdrawn, Balance).

Safaricom statements are locked. The password is the code in the SMS Safaricom sent when you requested the statement.

Ways to get your M-Pesa data in

  • From your phone: open the M-PESA thread in Messages, select the messages, copy them and send them to yourself (email, WhatsApp Web, notes). Then paste them here.
  • Messages for Web: open your SMS on the computer (for example Google Messages for web), highlight M-Pesa messages, right-click and choose Import selection into M-Pesa Ledger.
  • Full statement: request one in the M-PESA app (Statements) or with *334# → My Account → M-PESA Statement. Safaricom emails a password-protected PDF. Upload that PDF here and type the password from the SMS.
